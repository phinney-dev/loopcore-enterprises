PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy explains how Loopcore Enterprises ("Loopcore", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information relating to customers, website visitors, and users of our products and services.

By using our services, customers acknowledge that information may be collected, used, stored, and protected as described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable law.

1.1 We may collect information reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, improve, secure, and bill for our services. Such information may include customer identification details, contact information, installation addresses, billing records, payment information, technical service information, support communications, and other information voluntarily provided by customers.

2. Use of Information

Information may be used for service activation, installation, billing, customer support, network administration, service improvement, security monitoring, fraud prevention, business operations, compliance obligations, and communication regarding services, accounts, or important updates.

3.1 During normal operation of our internet and technology services, certain technical data may be generated automatically, including IP addresses, device identifiers, connection records, authentication logs, network diagnostics, and performance metrics. 4.1 Our website may use cookies, analytics tools, and similar technologies to improve user experience, enhance security, remember preferences, and analyze website performance. Users may adjust browser settings to manage or disable cookies, although certain website functions may be affected.

5.1 Loopcore Enterprises does not sell customer personal information. 6.1 We implement reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational measures intended to safeguard information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, misuse, loss, or destruction.
